We are authorised for four core activities, all limited to Professional clients and Eligible Counterparties.

Dealing in investments as agent — we can execute trades on behalf of your clients across regulated markets. Arranging

And managing investments — discretionary portfolio management.

Beyond that, we can set up fund structures. Crypto spot funds housed entirely by Odum, and derivatives plus traditional
