Notes & methodology

  • Brand-new epic, small by design. Carved out of infrastructure_master + client_isolation_and_governance_master on 2026-08-18 during the epic-taxonomy restructure. Content-based classification of ~300 candidate docs found only 5 whose PRIMARY subject is UAC's own schema/contract governance — a small bucket on purpose, not an oversight. This is the epic's first /plan-reconcile pass since the carve-out.
  • Counts are measured, not carried forward. Ledger totals (§01) and every open/done tally (§02) come from scripts/plan-hygiene/epic_report_data.py --epic uac_master --json, which scopes count_open_tasks.py's exact dedup methodology (aggregator-plan exclusion, identical checkbox regexes) to this epic's parent_epic: uac_master children — the same definition /open-task-count uses corpus-wide.
  • Membership is frontmatter-driven. "Child of this epic" means rg "^parent_epic: uac_master$" against real frontmatter, not the filename-substring heuristic regenerate_active_plan_inventory.py uses (that heuristic false-positives at a measured 3-of-4 rate on dispatch-batch plans — see epic_taxonomy_restructure_and_html_reconcile_2026_08_18.md).
  • Corpus-wide entry-state, not epic-specific. The read-only Phase-0 hygiene sweep (run_hygiene_sweep.sh --ci --no-regen) found 1 pre-existing hard failure (assigned_vm:NA corpus-size ratchet) and 1 soft warning (delete/VM-launch tagging) corpus-wide — neither traces to this epic's 4-5 docs specifically; both are /na-eligibility-audit's standing remit, not fixed here.
  • No sub-agent fan-out this pass. At 5 children, every doc was read in full directly rather than through Phase 1's usual parallel-hunter fan-out — the small-corpus path the skill itself calls out, not a shortcut.
