Notes & methodology

parent_epic membership is the frontmatter-driven definition (rg "^parent_epic: predictions_master$"), not the filename-substring inventory-script method — per the epic-taxonomy restructure's own finding, the substring method false-positives dispatch-batch plans as orphans purely because an epic's related_plans: list doesn't track batch churn.

This corpus has been reconciled daily for 3+ calendar days prior to this run (2026-08-16 through 2026-08-18) — the low finding count and 0 refuted candidates reflect that standing cadence, not a light pass. One of 5 dispatched hunters (covering the hub, Phase C, and batch 12 pair) hit a global concurrent-subagent ceiling from other active sessions on this shared host and never returned a result; its exact scope was covered directly instead, so coverage remains 18/18 (100%) of the active child docs.
