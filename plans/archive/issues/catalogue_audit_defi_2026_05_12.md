# Catalogue audit — defi (cross_asset_group plan)

## Methodology

Cross-referenced five layers per the parent plan: (1) UAC capability/venue/registry SSOTs
(`_defi.py:PROTOCOL_CAPABILITIES`/`SUBGRAPH_IDS`, `defi_venue_capabilities.py:DEFI_VENUE_DATA_TYPE_CAPABILITIES`,
`defi_venues.py:ALL_DEFI_VENUES`/`DEFI_VENUE_PHASE`/`MTDS_DEFI_VENUES`, `defi_protocol_registry.py`,
`market_data_categories.py:VENUES_BY_ASSET_GROUP`, `_defi_lst.py`, `_defi_oracle_coverage.py`,
`internal/domain/defi/lst.py`, `internal/risk.py:MarginModel`, `chain_env.py`); (2) UAC coverage windows
(`CHAIN_GENESIS_DATES`, `GAS_FEE_CHAIN_START_DATES`, `PROTOCOL_LAUNCH_DATES`, `LST_TOKEN_GENESIS`,
`ORACLE_COVERAGE_START`); (3) instruments-service `reference_data/factory.py` (`CANONICAL_VENUE_TO_ADAPTER`,
`_SUBGRAPH_VENUE_PREFIX_TO_PROTOCOL`, `_ADAPTERS`) + the 26 adapter files; (4) MTDS `market_interface/adapters/defi/`

- `cli/handlers/` (per data_type); (5) execution-service `defi_execution/protocols/` connector classes. For each
  protocol/venue I checked presence across all five layers + diffed casing/keys. Grep-then-read applied (opened
  `factory.py` build loops, `_defi.py` build helpers, adapter `__init__`/factory dicts, not just literal greps).
