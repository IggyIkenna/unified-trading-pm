Glossary — venue & instrument ID

Two terms carry a lot of weight below and mean something slightly different in each asset group. Worth pinning down first, because the normalization behind them is what makes one registry legible across five very different kinds of market.

Venue

Whose price is this — not necessarily somewhere we can trade. That's the literal operator ruling behind the registry (2026-08-08, sports-taxonomy decision): keep every real price source as a venue, then answer "can we act on it" with a separate flag. A venue can be data-only — PINNACLE is registered with no execution adapter at all — and it still counts as a venue.

Instrument ID — canonical, normalized

Every asset group's raw data passes through one dispatch point — build_canonical_instrument_id() — before anything downstream ever sees it. Its own docstring states the goal directly: "one builder for everything ... every asset group, every instrument type, can get its canonical instrument IDs ... just by filling in the right inputs." CeFi, DeFi, TradFi, and prediction markets share one shape, VENUE[-CHAIN]:INSTRUMENT_TYPE:SYMBOL — DeFi's venue is a protocol+chain pair (AAVE_V3-ARBITRUM:LENDING:USDC); sports keeps its own LEAGUE:MATCHUP:DATE fixture scheme; sports and prediction betting markets share a SPORT:VENUE:MARKET:LEAGUE:SEASON:HOME-AWAY::SELECTION grammar underneath that.

That last shape is what makes this real: the same Arsenal–Chelsea match, quoted on a sports betting exchange and on a prediction-market platform, produces two IDs identical in every field except venue — a genuine shipped example, not a mockup:

FOOTBALL:POLYMARKET:MATCH_ODDS:EPL:2025-26:ARSENAL-CHELSEA::HOME FOOTBALL:BETFAIR_EX_UK:MATCH_ODDS:EPL:2025-26:ARSENAL-CHELSEA::HOME

That's not a cosmetic parallel, it's the arb-detection mechanism itself: group by everything except venue, compare prices. Shipped and paper-proven, not aspirational — cross-venue-prediction-arb-detection.md, status SHIPPED since 2026-07-20 — normalizing Betfair's decimal back/lay odds onto the same YES-probability scale Kalshi and Polymarket already quote natively, then scanning every venue pair for a real edge. Kalshi shares the same outer VENUE:PREDICTION_MARKET:... shape with its own market-ticker grammar (KX{SERIES}-{DATE}-{STRIKE}) — same normalization discipline; we don't have a live production ticker to quote verbatim here, just the confirmed format.
