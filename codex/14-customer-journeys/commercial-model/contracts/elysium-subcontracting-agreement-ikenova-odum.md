# IkeNova x Odum Research — Subcontracting Agreement re Elysium (verbatim)

> **Provenance (2026-08-11).** Transcribed into codex on operator instruction. Source:
> `~/Downloads/NOVATION AGREEMENT IkeNova x Odum Research re Elysium.pdf`. Verbatim apart from page furniture.

## § It is a SUBCONTRACT, not a novation — and codex said otherwise

**The filename says "NOVATION AGREEMENT". The document does not.** It is titled **"SUBCONTRACTING AGREEMENT"** and its
operative terms are the opposite of a novation:

|                        | Novation (what the filename implies) | Subcontract (what the document does)                                            |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Elysium's counterparty | becomes Odum Research                | **stays IkeNova Ltd**                                                           |
| IkeNova's liability    | discharged                           | **"shall remain fully responsible for performance of all obligations"** (cl. 3) |
| Elysium's consent      | required, and evidenced              | **not evidenced anywhere in the instrument**                                    |

**Correction to
[`elysium-managed-sla-2026-05-14.md`](/codex/14-customer-journeys/commercial-model/elysium-managed-sla-2026-05-14.md) §1
and §7.** That doc states "IkeNova Ltd has migrated to Odum Research; SLA names Odum Research as Service Provider with
successor-entity clarification clause", and §7 says the SLA's legal-parties section should name IkeNova exactly as the
contract signature page while treating "Odum Research" as a marketing trade name. The §7 instruction is the one
consistent with this instrument; the §1 "migrated" framing is not. **On the documents as they stand, IkeNova Ltd is
still the Elysium counterparty.** Naming Odum Research as Service Provider in a client-facing SLA is therefore a
substantive entity change that needs either a real novation (with Elysium's written consent under Art. 7.7) or explicit
drafting that Odum Research contracts as IkeNova's subcontractor.

## § Open points on this instrument

1. **Unsigned and undated.** Dated "[Insert Date]"; both signature and date lines are blank. Until executed it evidences
   an intention, not an arrangement.
2. **Cites the contract as "dated 3 March 2025"** (twice) — consistent with the e-signed PDF, inconsistent with the
   `(w specifics)` DOCX's 1 March. See the consulting-agreement record's defect table.
3. **Article 1.4 consent gap.** Consulting Agreement Art. 1.4 permits outside services only "subject to the prior
   written consent of an officer of the Company", and requires the third party to execute a confidentiality agreement
   **before** any information is disclosed to it. Background B of this instrument cites Art. 1.4 as the permission, but
   no Elysium consent is attached or referenced. Clause 4 binds Odum Research to the same confidentiality and
   work-product terms, which addresses the second limb but not the consent limb.
4. **Both signatories are the same person** (Ikenna Igboaka, director of both entities), which the instrument
   acknowledges at Background D and clause 2. Self-dealing of this kind is ordinarily fine but is worth a written
   Elysium acknowledgement given the Art. 1.4 consent requirement.
5. **Governing law is BVI** here, whereas the Consulting Agreement is **Ireland** (Art. 7.2) with Irish Arbitration
   Centre dispute resolution (Art. 7.4).

## § Verbatim text

SUBCONTRACTING AGREEMENT

Relating to Consulting Agreement dated 3 March 2025

This Subcontracting Agreement (the "Agreement") is made on [Insert Date] by and between:

1. IkeNova Ltd, a company incorporated under the laws of the British Virgin Islands, having its registered office at OMC
   Chambers, Wickhams Cay 1, Road Town, Tortola, British Virgin Islands, VG1110, Incorporation Number 2169565 (“Main
   Contractor"); and

2. Odum Research Ltd, a company incorporated under the laws of the United Kingdom, having its registered office at 9
   Appold Street, London EC2A 2AP, United Kingdom, Incorporation Number 13531816 (“Subcontractor").

Collectively referred to as the Parties.

BACKGROUND

A. The Main Contractor is party to a Consulting Agreement dated 3 March 2025 with Elysium AM Ltd for the provision of
services related to delta-neutral cryptocurrency trading strategy development (the "Primary Contract").

B. Under Article 1.4 of the Primary Contract, the Main Contractor is permitted to subcontract services subject to
confidentiality and ownership obligations.

C. The Main Contractor wishes to subcontract to the Subcontractor certain responsibilities under the Primary Contract,
specifically those related to delivery of work and receipt of compensation, while remaining ultimately liable under the
Primary Contract.

D. Mr. Ikenna Igboaka, being the director of both the Main Contractor and the Subcontractor, confirms his authority to
enter into this arrangement and agrees to its terms.

TERMS OF SUBCONTRACTING

1. SUBCONTRACTING APPOINTMENT The Main Contractor hereby appoints the Subcontractor to perform services and receive
   associated fees on its behalf under the Primary Contract.

2. AUTHORISATION

Ikenna Igboaka, being a director of both parties, confirms that this subcontracting arrangement is duly authorized and
that all services performed by the Subcontractor will be treated as services delivered under the Primary Contract.

3. NO RELEASE FROM RESPONSIBILITIES

The Main Contractor shall remain fully responsible for performance of all obligations under the Primary Contract. This
Agreement does not modify or relieve any obligations owed to Elysium AM Ltd.

4. CONFIDENTIALITY AND WORK PRODUCT

The Subcontractor agrees to be bound by the same confidentiality and work product ownership terms set forth in the
Primary Contract.

5. GOVERNING LAW

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the British Virgin Islands.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties hereto have executed this Subcontracting Agreement as of the date first above written.
