# POST-DELIVERY SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT (SLA)

This Post-Delivery Service Level Agreement (the "SLA") is entered into by and between:

**Elysium AM Ltd.** (the "Client")

and

**Odum Research** (the "Service Provider", successor entity to IkeNova Ltd. named in the Consulting Agreement)

Effective Date: \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_

This SLA supplements the Consulting Agreement executed on **1 March 2025** between **Elysium AM Ltd.** and **IkeNova
Ltd.** relating to the design, development, implementation, and deployment of the Client's cryptocurrency delta-neutral
basis trading strategy infrastructure and associated DeFi/CeFi trading systems (the "Consulting Agreement"). The parties
confirm that Odum Research is the successor entity to IkeNova Ltd. and that all rights and obligations of IkeNova
Ltd. under the Consulting Agreement have been migrated to and are now held by Odum Research with the Client's
acknowledgement.

---

# 1. PURPOSE

The purpose of this SLA is to define the post-delivery support, operational assistance, maintenance, transition
services, and (where elected) ongoing managed-operation services to be provided by the Service Provider following the
completion and acceptance of the deliverables described in the Consulting Agreement.

This SLA establishes:

- The scope of post-delivery support and the locked Phase 2 acceptance scope;

- Response and communication expectations (24/7 commitment under the paid Managed Service tier);

- Operational assistance, 24/7 monitoring support, and incident-response procedures;

- Strategy scope precision and definition of the Work Product;

- Knowledge transfer and onboarding assistance;

- The two post-30-day continuation options: **Option A --- Managed Service** (Service Provider operates the Strategy)
  and **Option B --- Self-Run Carve-Out** (Client takes over operation);

- Service limitations and exclusions;

- Transition timelines (including a written acknowledgement of timeline relative to Annex A of the Consulting
  Agreement);

- Commercial terms for the paid Managed Service tier (fixed retainer + performance share);

- Carve-out hand-over scope and pricing (Option B);

- The exhibits forming part of this SLA: Exhibit A (Work Product Manifest), Exhibit B (Strategy Family Scope +
  Non-Compete Clarification), Exhibit C (Optional Pre-Cutover Integration Services).

---

# 2. RELATIONSHIP TO MASTER CONSULTING AGREEMENT

This SLA supplements the Consulting Agreement entered into between the parties and shall not replace or supersede any
confidentiality, intellectual property, ownership, or dispute resolution provisions contained therein.

All Work Product, systems, codebases, infrastructure configurations, strategy logic, dashboards, documentation,
deployment pipelines, and associated deliverables remain governed by the original Consulting Agreement. **Exhibit A
(Work Product Manifest)** enumerates the modules and artefacts that constitute Work Product delivered under the
Consulting Agreement and clarifies which Service Provider platform components are excluded from Work Product (as
pre-existing Service Provider platform IP, generic programming methods retained under Article 4.6 of the Consulting
Agreement, or multi-tenant infrastructure serving Service Provider's other engagements).

**Timeline acknowledgement**: The parties acknowledge that the indicative eight (8) month duration stated in Annex A §
Term of the Consulting Agreement was an expected, not contractual, completion timeframe. The parties confirm by mutual
continued performance that the Phase 2 production-acceptance event occurring on or around **June 2026** satisfies the
Consulting Agreement's Phase 2 acceptance criteria notwithstanding the indicative timeframe.

# 3. POST-DELIVERY SUPPORT PERIOD

Upon formal acceptance and delivery of the final production system (the "Acceptance Event"), the Service Provider shall
provide a structured post-delivery support period (the "Initial Support Period").

Unless otherwise agreed in writing:

- The Initial Support Period shall last for **thirty (30) calendar days** from the Acceptance Event, at no additional
  charge to the Client.

- During this period, the Service Provider shall provide operational guidance, troubleshooting assistance, clarification
  support, incident response, and reasonable remediation related to the delivered system.

- The purpose of the Initial Support Period is to ensure a stable operational transition and assist the Client in
  adopting the deployed infrastructure, or in preparing for either Option A (Managed Service) or Option B (Self-Run
  Carve-Out) continuation.

- **Exhibit C** describes optional Pre-Cutover Integration Services (Copper.co institutional custody integration and
  CEFFU institutional custody integration scheduled for June 2026) that the Service Provider shall provide at no
  additional charge to the Client as part of operational readiness prior to and during the Initial Support Period.

**Capital and timing**: For clarity, the Service Provider's pre-cutover testing through and including May 2026 is
conducted on the Service Provider's own capital and the Service Provider's own wallets (Trust Wallet under Cloud KMS
envelope encryption and equivalent CeFi sandbox accounts). The Service Provider does not request or accept Client
capital in this pre-cutover phase. The Client's seed capital, contributed under Annex A of the Consulting Agreement
("pilot execution with 'seed' capital provided by Company"), is provided **from 30 June 2026 onwards**, by which date
the Service Provider's pre-cutover testing on its own capital is expected to be complete and the Copper.co institutional
custody integration described in Exhibit C --- and the CEFFU integration where the Client has elected it --- is expected
to be live.

# 7. POST-INITIAL-SUPPORT-PERIOD CONTINUATION OPTIONS

At the end of the Initial Support Period, the Client shall elect one of two continuation options.

## 7.1 Option A --- Managed Service (default election absent written notice)

The Service Provider continues to operate the In-Scope Strategies 24/7 from Service Provider infrastructure on the
commercial terms set out in **§10**.

This option includes:

- Continued 24/7 monitoring + alert response per §6 (same Critical-1h / High-4h targets);

- Incident response and remediation;

- Bug fixes within the delivered Work Product;

- Custody and venue API maintenance (rotations, version upgrades within the existing scope);

- Monthly investor-style performance reporting;

- Up to ten (10) hours per calendar month of new end-client share-class / fund-structure onboarding effort at no
  additional charge; hours beyond ten (10) billed at \$200/hr at the preferred rate;

- Operational continuity through any custody-provider material change or venue API breaking change (first four (4) hours
  per calendar quarter included; beyond billed at \$200/hr).

This option excludes:

- New Strategy archetypes (separate SOW, see §4.3);

- New venues, new Liquid Staking Tokens, new chains (per-event one-time fees, see §4.3);

- Significant Strategy parameter re-design;

- Reg-reporting infrastructure for TradFi venue additions;

- Anything in §9 (Exclusions from Included Support);

- The Service Provider's multi-tenant platform UI access (the platform stays Service Provider--side; Client receives
  monthly reports + alert-channel access only).

## 7.2 Option B --- Self-Run Carve-Out

The Client elects to take operational responsibility for the In-Scope Strategies on Client-controlled infrastructure.

This option includes a **one-time hand-over package**, comprising the operationally-complete software stack required for
the Client to run the In-Scope Strategies on its own infrastructure:

- **Source-code repository access** to the Work Product enumerated in **Exhibit A § A.1** (the strategy engines, the
  strategy configurations for the In-Scope venues, the execution adapters for Copper.co + CEFFU + OKX + Bybit +
  Binance + Lido, and the strategy-specific monitoring panels and reporting templates);

- **A frozen snapshot of the Licensed Components enumerated in Exhibit A § A.3** (the unified-api-contracts runtime
  subset, the unified-trading-library runtime subset, the unified-cloud-interface runtime subset, the features-onchain
  calculator subset, and the instruments-service reference-data subset, in each case as required for the In-Scope
  Strategies to function in live operation), delivered under the perpetual non-exclusive non-transferable licence in §
  A.3;

- **A lightweight CLI-runner deployment runbook** (a single binary or container-image execution path; no Service
  Provider UI, no Service Provider dashboards, no multi-tenant client portal);

- **Strategy-config templates** for the In-Scope venues and Liquid Staking Tokens;

- **Operational runbook** for the In-Scope venues, Liquid Staking Tokens, custody providers (Copper.co + CEFFU), and the
  Ethereum RPC endpoint configuration;

- **Four (4) hours of Q&A and live walkthrough** with the Client's engineering team, scheduled at a mutually agreed time
  within thirty (30) calendar days of Option B election.

**One-time hand-over and training fee: USD \$10,000**, payable on Option B election. The fee covers the Service
Provider's effort to (a) prepare the frozen snapshot of the Licensed Components, (b) prepare the lightweight CLI-runner
deployment runbook, (c) prepare the strategy-config and operational runbooks, and (d) deliver the four-hour walkthrough.

Under Option B, the Client is responsible for **all of**: deployment, CI/CD pipeline setup, secrets management,
infrastructure provisioning (cloud accounts, VMs, networking, monitoring stack), security posture, alerting, on-call
rotation, custody-provider operational relationships, venue operational relationships, regulatory reporting
infrastructure, fund-administration interfaces, and ongoing Strategy parameter tuning. The Service Provider has no
ongoing operational obligations under Option B.

Performance share under **§10.2 survives election of Option B** and continues to be payable for as long as the In-Scope
Strategies are operated for any end-client of the Client.

Post hand-over, any further Service Provider involvement (consulting, debugging, upgrades, new venues) shall be billed
at **USD \$300/hr** with a four-hour minimum per request and may be subject to capacity.

## 7.3 Election, default, and switching

- **Election deadline**: Client shall notify Service Provider of its election in writing no later than ten (10) business
  days before the end of the Initial Support Period.

- **Default**: absent written election, **Option A (Managed Service) auto-applies from Day 61**.

- **Switching A → B**: permitted at any time on 30 days' written notice; \$10,000 hand-over and training fee becomes due
  on election.

- **Switching B → A**: permitted but requires re-onboarding of the codebase to Service Provider infrastructure; billed
  at **USD \$5,000** one-time + 30-day re-onboarding delay.

# 10. COMMERCIAL TERMS --- MANAGED SERVICE TIER (OPTION A)

From the start of Day 61 (or Day 1 of any agreed extension), the Managed Service tier carries the following commercial
terms.

## 10.1 Fixed Monthly Retainer

**USD \$3,000 per calendar month**, payable in advance on the first business day of each calendar month.

The retainer covers:

- 24/7 monitoring + incident response per §6;

- AI-assisted anomaly triage and code-repair drafting layer;

- L1 monitoring operator + L2 engineering escalation cover;

- Up to ten (10) hours per calendar month of new end-client share-class / fund onboarding effort;

- First four (4) hours per calendar quarter of unplanned custody-provider or venue API breaking-change response;

- Monthly investor-style performance reporting;

- Managed cloud infrastructure (compute, storage, network, security) sized to AUM \< USD \$50,000,000 allocated to the
  In-Scope Strategies (see §10.4 for scaling tiers).

The retainer is reviewed semi-annually (every six (6) calendar months from the Acceptance Event) based on the parameters
in §10.4.

## 10.2 Performance Share

In addition to the fixed retainer, the Service Provider shall be entitled to a tiered share of the gross performance
fees received by the Client (or any affiliate of the Client) from end-clients in respect of allocations to the In-Scope
Strategies, calculated as follows on the Client's Strategy-attributable AUM at the start of each calendar month:

---

Strategy-attributable AUM Performance share rate tranche (USD)

---

The first \$100,000,000 of **25%** of gross performance AUM fees attributable to that tranche

Above \$100,000,000 of AUM **10%** of gross performance fees attributable to that tranche
-----------------------------------------------------------

The tranching is applied to gross performance fees on a per-tranche basis (i.e. waterfall): if Strategy-attributable AUM
in a month is, for example, \$150M, then 25% applies to the gross performance fees attributable to the first \$100M and
10% applies to the gross performance fees attributable to the remaining \$50M.

Reporting and payment mechanic:

- The Client's fund administrator (a registered, regulated fund administrator engaged by the Client) shall produce a
  monthly statement of (a) Strategy-attributable AUM at the start of the calendar month and (b) gross performance fees
  received from end-clients in respect of allocations to CARRY_STAKED_BASIS and / or CARRY_BASIS_PERP (the "Reported
  Performance Fees"), broken down by AUM tranche.

- The Client shall make this monthly statement available to the Service Provider via the fund administrator's reporting
  portal (or, where portal access is not technically practical, via email in PDF or CSV form) within ten (10) business
  days of the end of each calendar month.

- The Service Provider may cross-verify Reported Performance Fees against the trade-level records the Service Provider
  holds for the In-Scope Strategies (including via venue API keys held by the Service Provider for accounts trading the
  In-Scope Strategies).

- The Service Provider shall invoice the applicable tranche-weighted share of the Reported Performance Fees within five
  (5) business days of receipt of the monthly statement.

- Payment is due within twenty (20) calendar days of invoice.

- The retainer (§10.1) and performance share (§10.2) are **independent flows**; the performance share does not offset or
  reduce the retainer.

**Survival**:

- Under **Option A** (Managed Service), the performance-share obligation continues for as long as the Service Provider
  continues to operate the In-Scope Strategies under this SLA.

- Under **Option B** (Self-Run Carve-Out), the performance-share obligation continues for **twenty-four (24) calendar
  months from the effective date of Option B election**, for as long as the In-Scope Strategies are operated for any
  end-client of the Client during that 24-month window. After the 24-month survival window, no further performance-share
  is due in respect of Option B operation.

**Audit right**: the Service Provider has the right, on ten (10) business days' written notice to the Client, to
commission a third-party auditor to verify the Reported Performance Fees during any rolling twelve (12) month period.
Audit costs are borne by the Service Provider unless the audit finds more than five per cent (5%) under-reporting, in
which case the Client bears audit costs plus pays the back-payment of the under-reported amount plus ten per cent (10%)
interest. Where the fund administrator portal provides sufficient self-service reconciliation, no separate audit is
required.

**No cap, no floor, no high-water mark on the Service Provider side**: the performance share is a flat tranche-weighted
percentage flow-through on whatever the Client actually receives. The Client's commercial structure with its end-clients
(high-water marks, hurdle rates, fee tier breakpoints) is at the Client's discretion.

## 10.3 One-Time Expansion Fees

In addition to the retainer and performance share, the Client may from time to time commission expansion work per §4.3
at the one-time fees stated there.

## 10.4 Capital Scaling Tiers

The fixed retainer in §10.1 assumes the Client's AUM allocated to the In-Scope Strategies stays below USD \$50,000,000.
Beyond that threshold, the retainer scales as follows:

---

AUM allocated to In-Scope Retainer multiplier Strategies (USD)

---

\< \$50,000,000 1.0× (\$3,000/mo)

\$50,000,000 -- \$100,000,000 1.25×

\$100,000,000 -- 1.5× \$250,000,000

\$250,000,000 -- 2.0× \$500,000,000

\> \$500,000,000 Bespoke negotiation
-----------------------------------------------------------

Tiers are re-assessed at each semi-annual retainer review based on average AUM over the trailing six months.

## 10.5 Inflation

The retainer (§10.1) and rates (\$200/hr preferred rate, \$300/hr post-Option-B rate, \$250/hr forensics rate) are
reviewed annually on each anniversary of the Acceptance Event and may be adjusted by the lesser of (a) UK CPI plus 1%,
or (b) 5%, as published for the preceding twelve (12) months. The performance-share percentage (25%) is not subject to
inflation adjustment.

## 10.6 Currency, taxes, and invoicing

- All amounts are stated in **US Dollars (USD)** unless expressly noted.

- The Service Provider is not currently above the United Kingdom value added tax registration threshold and is not
  registered for VAT; accordingly, no VAT is charged on invoices issued under this SLA. The Service Provider shall
  notify the Client in writing if its VAT registration status changes during the term of this SLA, in which case future
  invoices may include VAT at the prevailing rate.

- Any non-VAT taxes lawfully chargeable in respect of services provided under this SLA (e.g. withholding taxes
  deductible at source by the Client under applicable law) shall be the responsibility of the Client; the Service
  Provider may issue grossed-up invoices where necessary to preserve the agreed retainer and performance-share amounts
  net of any such withholding.

- Invoices are sent electronically to the Client's nominated accounts-payable email address.

- Late payment carries interest at the rate set by the Central Bank of Ireland for commercial late payment, or the
  European Central Bank Main Refinancing Rate plus 8%, whichever is higher.
